# Cross-lineage audit of the 14 October calibrated-answers session

A session on a different model family audited the calibrated-answers PR
(head `9f6e45f`) before merge. It ran the checks independently, from committed
inputs and fresh downloads (deleted afterwards). This file records what it
found and what was changed in review. The ledger and STATUS cite it. It is not
a second scientific examination of GOODS-N; the auditor recomputed from
committed compact products only.

## Verified

- **Freeze and access chronology.** The freeze bundle restores into a fresh
  bare repository (14 refs, 22 commits). All ten freeze-before-access pairs
  hold against the raw receipts, not only `lead_validation.json`. GS spectra
  were fetched before the prediction commit `4fbf419`. GOODS-N was first
  touched after it, by arm B only. No C/D/E receipt mentions GOODS-N. Every
  `docs/frozen/*` file and every frozen draw/prediction is byte-identical to
  its freeze commit. The frozen GS draw (1989 eligible) and GN draw (1227
  eligible) re-derive exactly from the public catalogues.
- **Numbers.**
  - Every population pull recomputes with zero replay difference.
  - The MoM 41-node interval reproduces as [0.1015474, 13.4804285], and the
    81-node diagnostic reproduces as well.
  - The SMACS compact replay matches byte for byte apart from its access
    timestamp. Its gate decisions are unchanged.
  - The combined-error pull −1.2094 recomputes.
- **Budgets.** Per-arm totals recomputed from the journals match
  `DATA_BUDGET.md` and the PR table.
- **Governance.** The quality gate exits 0 (841 PASS, 3 allow-listed SKIP).
  No row is Candidate or Validated.
- **SMACS and YSO.** No same-photon replay is credited as independent.
  Unknown coverage is never treated as zero flux. The cited ice-variability
  literature is real and accurately quoted.
- **Published MoM-z14 claim.** The claim (arXiv:2505.11263v2) is located and
  quoted accurately. The comparison is ionic against ionic with the same
  lines.

## Findings and what changed

1. **The population row was graded as a lead; it is a rejected hypothesis plus
   a calibration finding.**
   - The tested claim was agreement with JADES DR4 within quoted errors, and
     it is now graded Rejected.
   - The test also shows a replicated systematic flux deficit:
     - median pipeline/JADES ratio 0.92 in GS and 0.86 in GN;
     - 13 of 14 GN pulls are negative (sign test p=0.002);
     - the GN mean is consistent with the GS training mean (Welch p=0.47);
     - the deficit is insensitive to widening the width cap (3 to 8 bins)
       and the centroid prior (±1 to ±4 px).
   - The GS training mean already sat outside the frozen mean interval
     (t-test p=0.045). The narrow GN SD is the expected shared-photon
     signature and carries no information.
2. **The 1.2094 "pass" is not passing evidence.**
   - The criterion was set after the result was known.
   - The combined-error denominator ignores positive shared-photon
     covariance, so it leans toward passing.
   - The comparison is 1.89σ under the original published-error criterion,
     in the same direction as the deficit.
   - STATUS and the methods note now say so. The frozen amendment file is
     unchanged.
3. **The MoM-z14 PRISM interval cannot discriminate.**
   - Only the upper end (about 13) is robust. The lower end moves under
     different interval methods: exact-grid Fieller [0.10157, 13.476];
     Gaussian Monte Carlo [0.029, 13.0]; full-mixture Monte Carlo
     [−0.044, 13.06].
   - Wavelength-prior nodes carrying 65% of the weight exclude the ambient
     reference. It enters only through nodes with offset u ≥ 0.15, so the
     prior, not the data, decides ambient membership.
   - Every node admits the headline boundary 2.51, so PRISM photons cannot
     test [N/C]>1.
   - About 18% of repeated draws at this noise level would give unbounded
     Fieller sets. The code handles them correctly.
   - The arm is paused until G235H data (NIV] at 2.30 µm and CIII] at
     2.95 µm for z=14.44).
4. **The methods note had a stale section.** "Conditional spectroscopy" said
   no fresh interval existed, and is now updated.
5. **Nits.**
   - The GN timeout was a spectrum GET, not a HEAD (fixed in
     `CALIBRATED_KNOWN_ANSWER.md`).
   - `b_northern_confirmation.json` labels the GN sample `"half": "training"`.
     It is left unchanged because the artifact is hashed.
   - E_association's commit and access fall in the same second, so
     `lead_validation.json`'s `pass` for that pair cannot be shown from
     timestamps.
   - All timestamps are self-asserted local clocks.

## MoM-z14 fresh line fluxes against the published table (nit, recorded)

Fresh fluxes are `fluxes[i]` in
`research_output/calibrated_20261014/c_replay41.json`, with errors equal to
the square root of the covariance diagonal. Published fluxes are from
arXiv:2505.11263v2 Table 3. All fluxes are in units of 10^-20 erg/s/cm².
Pulls use the mean of the asymmetric published errors and ignore
shared-photon covariance, so they are rough.

| Line | Fresh | Published | Fresh / published | Combined pull |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NIV] 1487 | 25.594 ± 12.268 | 54.3 +16.1/−15.5 | 0.471 | −1.43 |
| CIV 1548,1551 | 22.730 ± 10.411 | 44.0 ± 12.8 | 0.517 | −1.29 |
| HeII+OIII] (not in N/C) | 18.160 ± 11.980 | 45.3 +15.6/−14.8 | 0.401 | −1.42 |
| NIII] 1747–1754 | 6.226 ± 7.715 | 25.2 +9.9/−8.9 | 0.247 | −1.56 |
| CIII] 1907,1909 | 13.179 ± 6.646 | 21.5 +7.7/−7.6 | 0.613 | −0.82 |

No single line is significantly discrepant, and fresh NIII] is not detected
(S/N 0.8). All five fresh fluxes are lower than the published values, by a
factor of about 0.25–0.6. That is the same direction as the population
deficit, but larger, and the lines share continuum and calibration, so the
five pulls are not independent.

**Closure check.** The repo's emissivities (Te = 20,000 K,
ne = 1000 cm⁻³, from `emissivity_json` in `c_completed_compact.npz`) applied
to the *published* fluxes give N/C = 5.524 (log 0.742). That is inside the
paper's 0.7–1.1, so the repo's physics reproduces the published ratio and the
difference lies in the fluxes. Resolving the flux-scale question, starting
with the population deficit above, comes before any further MoM inference.
